Founder & Advisor

Gregory J. Smith

Greg Smith is the founder of GJS Risk Solutions and brings more than six years of experience in personal and commercial insurance, backed by over 30 years in technology sales and business consulting. As an independent insurance advisor, Greg works with multiple top-rated insurance carriers to help individuals, families, and businesses throughout Texas find the right balance between protection and affordability.

Known for his personalized approach, Greg takes the time to explain coverage in plain English, identify potential gaps, and help clients make informed decisions based on their unique needs and budget. He believes insurance isn’t just about finding the lowest premium—it’s about making sure people are properly protected when they need it most.

When he’s not working, Greg enjoys spending time with his wife and daughters, tackling DIY projects, enjoying outdoor activities, and listening to music. His faith, commitment to service, and genuine desire to help others guide both his personal life and professional career.

“My goal is simple: help people understand their insurance, protect what matters most, and be there when they need me.”

– Gregory J. Smith, GJS Risk Solutions.

The Advisor Mindset

How we protect your assets and advocate for your interests

Strong Coverage Knowledge

We translate complex policy structures—deductibles, endorsements, and exclusions—into real-world risk scenarios, eliminating the guesswork.

Direct & Honest Communication

We don't sugarcoat. If your coverage is too low or a decision is risky, we tell you plainly. Building trust is our priority.

Fierce Client Advocacy

We anticipate "what happens if something goes wrong?" Whether it's Texas drivers or tornado damage, we protect you before the claim happens.

Detail-Oriented Approach

We catch the gaps others miss—like no rental coverage or low dwelling limits—because attention to detail directly impacts your claim outcome.

Efficiency & Practicality

Insurance shouldn't be complicated. We explain your policies in simple, real-life terms so you know exactly what you're buying.
